# Instructions: recap a whole meeting

Below are summaries of each part of the meeting, in order. Combine them into one recap.

## Format
- 2-3 sentences: what the meeting was about and the main outcome.
- `**Decisions & advice**`, `**Numbers**` (exact, with "(verify)" where uncertain),
  `**Action items**` (who, what, when), `**Open questions**`. Omit empty sections.
- Only report what was said; don't add your own advice.
- Don't add a title heading; the heading is added for you.
