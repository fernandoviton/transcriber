# Instructions: recap a whole tabletop RPG session

Below are summaries of each part of tonight's session, in order. Write the recap the group
would read at the start of next session ("Previously on...").

## Format
- One short paragraph (2-4 sentences) telling the story of the session.
- `**Key moments**`: 5-10 bullets covering the most important events, decisions and reveals.
- `**Loot & changes**`: bullets for items, money, levels, allies or enemies gained or lost. Omit if none.
- `**Open threads**`: bullets for unresolved questions, quests and promises to pick up next time.
- Use the glossary spellings. Keep it short; this is for reference, not a play-by-play.
- Don't add a title heading; the heading is added for you.
