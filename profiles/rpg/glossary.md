# Glossary

## Player characters

- Kerri: psychic with mind-reading, illusion, brain-blast and telekinetic powers; communes with hollows for visions of the past; hosts the ghost girl Alice; Toner in her body makes sunlight sicken her; also spelled "Keri" or "Carrie"
- Finn: brawler and talker with fans; carries dynamite, makes C4 animal "gifts", took the Shambling Tonerbot's buzzsaw arms
- Holly: female gunslinger and scout, aka Holly Day / Kid Holiday, a fan of Finn's; Toner scarred her face and arm, and she now has nanite skin and facial ridges; also spelled "Hollie"
- Thorne: fighter with spear and rifle; former captive of Dugan's Gang, wanted in Ft. St. John; also spelled "Thorn"
- Gibson: tech expert who opens hatches and consoles; nanite-covered hands and a robotic voice after the time leaps
- Skeleton: ranger guide who seeks revenge; stealthy tracker, healer and crafter; bonded with the Banshee Axe
- Laurent: former party member who can open lockers; exposed the Abbess as an android; killed by Miguel
- Cliff: former party member with Tier 2 voice access; jury-rigs power; killed by Miguel
- Floyd: helped Laurent expose the Abbess (open question: PC or NPC?)

## NPCs

- Alice: ghost girl who lives in Kerri's mind and can possess other bodies
- The Abbess: leader of the Abbey; an android from the Phoenix Project who woke about 350 years ago and is "dying"
- Effie: girl the Stalk resurrects every time she dies; the party's is Effie 25; left at the Abbey; also "Elfie"
- Effie's Mom: woman from the Hephaestus Space Station seen carved in various places; possibly the Stalk
- Alcalde Don Luis: ruler of Ft. St. John who seized the Abbey; can project mental images to his soldiers
- The Lieutenant: Don Luis's officer who led the attack on the Abbey
- Dugan: drunk but successful bandit leader out of Ft. St. John; left alive
- Billy: fairly smart member of Dugan's Gang; left alive
- Miguel: Dr. Hooch's adopted son, rode with Dugan's Gang; Toner-corrupted sniper with claw hands; killed Laurent and Cliff; dead
- Dr. Hooch: built the fort at Hooch's on a hollow; his family are were-yetis; known for his whisky
- Tasha: Dr. Hooch's daughter
- Farseer 5: whale-cult spokesperson who set Kerri's swimming challenge; also spelled "Farseerer 5"
- The Priestess: whale-cult leader on Moore Hill who guards the Depths
- The Great Mother: the whale cult's deity; probably an enormous Toner creature under Moore Hill
- Damocles: computer system in the fake "government house" near Deadtown
- RETAIN: system in Damocles that issued the final recommendations (open question)

## Places

- The Abbey: fortified religious refuge built up by the Abbess; now taken over by Don Luis
- The Phoenix Room: hidden chamber in the Abbey where the Abbess woke; a Phoenix cache
- Ft. St. John: Don Luis's town and Dugan's Gang's base; also "St. Johns"
- Hooch's: Dr. Hooch's fort, a former church; lights hold off Toner swarms; survived the disease and reopened
- The dam: site of a Phoenix Pod full of Toner Flybots
- The Wheel: half of the Hephaestus Space Station, surrounded by Boom Bugs; includes the Data Evaluation Lab, dispensary and Isolation Chamber
- Hephaestus Space Station: station built after a 1967 radio detection; its other half crashed into Deadtown; also spelled "Hephasteus"
- Deadtown: crater where the station half crashed and where the time loop was centered; now a hollow; also "Dead Town"
- The government house: fake house with antennas near Deadtown; a temple to the Great Mother that hides Damocles
- Moore Hill: whale-cult peak and temple; a shaft beneath it leads to the Depths
- The Depths: underground temple where the Great Mother lives; entrants are locked in for a day
- The island: small rocky island off the black-sand coast with a hatch and a low-power computer
- Prime Base: facility named by Damocles (open question)
- The Wisconsin: submarine that fired the Trident missile at Deadtown

## Other terms

- Phoenix Project: pre-fall program behind the Abbess, the caches and the pods
- Phoenix Pod: sealed cache; the pod people inside were all dead
- Phoenix packets: carried by Kerri and Finn; their codes opened Damocles's hatch
- Tier 2: access level that gives voice control of Phoenix systems
- Toner: nanite swarm that infests creatures and the sky; defines the "Toner era" the party comes from
- Toner disease: infection that may unlock psychic powers
- The Stalk: Suspected Toner entity hive mind
- Stalk: Suspected Toner entity hive mind
- Nanites: the microscopic machines behind Toner
- Toner swarms: clouds of Toner drawn by combat and at night
- Toner beast: large creature built from Toner that grows by absorbing weapons
- Toner Flybots: flying Toner drones found in Phoenix pods; also "Flyboys"
- Toner dogs: Toner-infested dogs
- Shambling Tonerbot: Toner robot with buzzsaw-firing arms
- Hollow: safe resting zone that shrinks over time; also "hallow" or "harrow"
- Clickers: big roach-like creatures
- Massive Merged Clicker: giant fused clicker found in a cave
- Boom Bugs: giant insects that explode in chain reactions; also "giant wasps"
- Jellyfish monsters: floating creatures that stay near water
- Were-yetis: Hooch's family's secret; the source of the sasquatch footprints
- Headhunter bots: flying robots that behead people and carry the heads off; also "beheading drones", "head cutter bots"
- Banshee Axe: made from a Toner Flybot power source; only its owner can hold it; a stunning glow that hurts Toner and cured Toner disease; broke at the Wheel
- Hornet spear: stinger spear that grafts to its wielder; left behind
- HUp1: healing packets from the Wheel's dispensary
- QUYX: unidentified packet type from the Wheel
- Caution X H2O: unidentified packet type from the Wheel
- Radiation pills: 24-hour anti-radiation doses from the Abbey
- Dugan's Gang: bandits out of Ft. St. John
- Whale cult: seal/whale tribe that eats blubber and worships the Great Mother; also "seal people" and "cultists"
- Blubber: whale-cult food and the test for joining
- The Cycle: time loop that reset whenever the missile struck Deadtown
- The Great Light: the cult's name for the nuclear blast
- The Mother's child: the cult's name for the missile
- Time leap: jump between eras inside the time bubble (1970s Christmas Eve, Cretaceous, Wild West, robot war)
- Time bubble: area around Deadtown caught in the Cycle; ended when Gibson shorted out the dark sphere
- Dark sphere: glowing ball of "darkness" from a canister in the T. rex's stomach
- Toner T. rex: nanite-infested T. rex with laser eyes that time-leapt with the canister
- Trident missile: nuclear missile from the Wisconsin
- Savage Worlds: game system (Shaken, wounds, natural healing)
- Shaken: Savage Worlds condition
